Powered land for data centers, by region

How the Midwest, Plains, Southwest and Mid-Atlantic compare when you are looking for land with a power pathway, and where NLRC sources sites.

Request Powered Land Opportunities
The short answer

NLRC supports data center projects throughout the Midwest, Plains, Southwest and Mid-Atlantic, as well as strong projects nationwide. Each region trades off differently on parcel size, power availability, water and proximity to existing markets, so NLRC evaluates every site individually rather than by region.

How the four regions compare

The characteristics below are general orientation for developers, not claims about any specific NLRC site. Availability of power, gas, fiber and water varies parcel by parcel inside every region.

RegionTypically attractive forTypically evaluated carefully
MidwestLarge agricultural parcels, established transmission and interstate natural gas corridors, and workforce familiar with heavy industryLocal zoning posture, fiber diversity at rural sites, and winter conditions

Where NLRC’s pipeline example sits

NLRC’s published pipeline example is a 620-acre contiguous Central Plains agricultural parcel with a 345 kV transmission line 0.4 miles away, a substation 1.1 miles away, a 16-inch interstate natural gas pipeline 0.8 miles away and long-haul fiber within 1.2 miles. It scored 96 out of 120 on NLRC’s framework and is assessed as capable of a 150 to 400 MW initial deployment. See the full example site profile.

How to use this when choosing a region

Start with power and interconnection timing, then work outward to gas, fiber, water, zoning and title. The data center site selection checklist lays out those criteria in order. If you already know your target regions and power needs, send them to Brian and NLRC will match you against what is in the pipeline.

Regional questions answered

Which regions does NLRC source powered land in?

NLRC supports projects throughout the Midwest, Plains, Southwest and Mid-Atlantic, as well as strong projects nationwide. Its published pipeline example is a Central Plains agricultural parcel.

Why do data center developers look beyond the biggest existing markets?

Established data center markets are increasingly constrained on available power and land. Developers therefore evaluate regions where large parcels, transmission corridors and natural gas infrastructure are more available, which is why NLRC concentrates on infrastructure first and acreage second.

Does NLRC have sites in a specific state?

Site locations are kept anonymized until a developer engages. Share your target states or regions through the request form and Brian Beauchamp will tell you what fits.
